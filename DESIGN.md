## Brand & Style
The design system targets high-intent property seekers, real estate investors, and luxury brokers who expect absolute clarity, high aesthetic integrity, and rapid discovery. It blends the intuitive hospitality-led browsing dynamics of contemporary travel apps with the restrained prestige of prime architectural monographs.

The emotional signature is grounded, authoritative, and aspirational. The UI stays whisper-quiet to give maximum resonance to architectural photography, utilizing generous whitespace, deliberate typographic cadence, and restrained gold accents to evoke discernment rather than flashiness.

### Design Movement
- **Modern Architectural Minimalism:** Prioritizes structured vertical flow, rich high-resolution imagery, subtle micro-borders, and tactile layered surfaces.
- **Utilitarian Elegance:** Complex real estate criteria (lot size, floor plates, zoning, structural history) are organized with clinical discipline and legible typographical hierarchy.

## Colors
The palette balances deep nautical architectural darks with a warm amber signature that signals value, exclusivity, and key conversion paths.

- **Primary (`#0A192F`):** Deep Navy/Obsidian. Governs primary interactive elements, critical headers, navigation anchors, and deep brand contrast.
- **Secondary (`#D97706`):** Warm Amber Gold. Reserved for luxury highlight tiers, featured listing callouts, verified agent credentials, and prime conversion actions.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`):** Refined Cobalt. Used sparingly for infrastructural utilities, interactive map markers, and status indicators.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Slate Core. Generates balanced low-contrast borders, secondary metadata labels, iconography, and subtle page canvas fills (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Canvas:** Crisp architectural white (`#FFFFFF`) with borders defined in subtle slate tints (`#E2E8F0` at 80% opacity) ensures high legibility against photography.

## Typography
Typography is paired to deliver high technical legibility and editorial rhythm:
- **Headlines and Values (`Geist`):** Delivers clean geometry, tight apertures, and balanced numerical figures crucial for valuation data, square footages, and street addresses.
- **Body & Continuous Text (`Inter`):** Selected for high x-height, neutral tone, and clarity in descriptive property briefs and disclosure tables.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** All currency values strictly utilize tabular or semi-bold variants of `Geist` to prevent optical misalignment during vertical scanning. Uppercase treatments are restricted strictly to `label-sm` for category pills, badge metadata, and floor-plan specs.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive 12-column grid system bounded by maximum content constraints:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 32px gutters, 48px outer canvas margin, maximum container width of 1360px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters, 24px outer canvas margin. Split screens for synchronized list-and-map viewports.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px outer canvas margin. Edge-to-edge media carousels bypass outer margins via negative offsets to prioritize tactile engagement.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical rhythmic pacing relies on strict 8pt base multiples (`space-xs`: 4px, `space-sm`: 8px, `space-md`: 16px, `space-lg`: 24px, `space-xl`: 40px). Micro spacing between metadata (e.g., bed/bath counters) stays locked to 8px, while content modules breathe with 40px separators.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through ambient light dispersion rather than hard boundaries, replicating clean museum-grade gallery lighting.

- **Level 0 (Flat/Base Canvas):** Background fill `#F8FAFC` paired with `#FFFFFF` panels separated by 1px borders colored `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 1 (Property Cards & Carousels):** Ambient suspension via `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.05)`. Communicates stable resting state.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Active Units):** Lifted state via `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.08)`. Border subtly deepens to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Search Bar & Bottom Action Sheets):** Floating functional widgets utilize `box-shadow: 0 20px 30px -10px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.12)` complemented by a glass backing (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with 85% opacity `#FFFFFF`).
- **Level 4 (Drawers & Full Filter Modals):** Backdrop overlay with 40% `#0A192F` opacity blur, supporting a raised bottom sheet or centered luxury dialog.

## Shapes
A roundedness tier of `2` (base radius of 8px / `0.5rem`) balances structural architectural rigidity with contemporary mobile ergonomics.

- **Standard Cards & Image Frames:** `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`), providing a refined framing for architectural images without cutting off aspect ratios.
- **Form Inputs, Filter Pods, and Segmented Controls:** 8px to 12px for clear tactile affordance.
- **Interactive Action Pills (Tags, Micro Badges):** Fully rounded capsules (`pill`) for high-contrast scanning against rectangular images.
- **Floating Modals & Bottom Sheets:** 24px top border radii for comfortable thumb interaction on mobile devices.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Luxury Action (Amber):** Solid `#D97706` background, crisp `#FFFFFF` typography, 12px radius, subtle active compression scale (0.98). Used strictly for "Schedule Tour", "Contact Exclusive Agent", and search execution.
- **Secondary Structural Action (Navy):** Solid `#0A192F` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Used for main site navigation, account creation, and primary modal triggers.
- **Tertiary/Ghost Actions:** 1px border `#E2E8F0`, transparent background, `#0A192F` text, transitioning on hover to `#F1F5F9`.

### Listing Card Patterns
- **Media Presentation:** 16:10 ratio with 16px corner radiuses. Image carousels include pagination indicator pills overlaid in translucent dark slate (`rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.6)`) with high-contrast white dots.
- **Heart / Save Action:** Floating circular button (36px diameter) top-right, frosted white background with 1px border `#FFFFFF40`, containing an SVG bookmark/heart icon.
- **Card Content Structure:** Tiered vertical stack:
  1. Price string (`price-lg`) alongside property verification badge.
  2. Spec line (`3 Beds • 2.5 Baths • 2,400 sqft`) formatted in `label-md` with slate tinting.
  3. Physical street address (`body-sm`) in neutral text.

### Floating Search & Filter Bar
- Sits pinned to top navigation on desktop, transitions to a floating capsule docked 16px above the bottom viewport on mobile.
- Features unified input partitions with vertical hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`).
- Integrated filter badge displays numeric active filter counts in `#D97706`.

### Agent Spotlights & Direct Booking Modules
- Compact profile card with 48px circular verified portrait, name, brokerage, performance badges (e.g., "Top 1% Producer"), and split-action triggers ("Call", "Direct Message").
- Uses Level 1 elevation with an interior `#F8FAFC` subtle fill to separate agent credentials from property data.

### Form Inputs & Inspection Switches
- **Inputs:** Height 48px, 10px radius, 1px `#CBD5E1` resting border, shifting to 1.5px `#0A192F` on active focus. 
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Customized with 4px inner rounding for checkboxes and full circular format for radio controls, highlighted with `#D97706` accent checks.
